# Backgammon

Jade Court plays one unranked, no-wager game for two seats. Invite one friend or play the bot.
The first player to bear off all fifteen checkers wins. There is no doubling cube, gammon or backgammon bonus, or match scoring.

## Play

- Each player starts with two checkers on their own 24-point, five on their 13-point, three on their 8-point, and five on their 6-point. The board shows a fixed numbering from Ivory’s perspective: Ivory moves toward 1 and Umber toward 24.
- The server rolls one die for each player to open, rerolling ties. The higher roller goes first and plays the two numbers already rolled. On subsequent turns, choose **Roll dice**. A double gives four separate moves of that value.
- Select a highlighted checker and then a highlighted destination. Two or more opposing checkers block a point; a single opposing checker is hit and sent to the bar. A player with checkers on the bar must enter them into the opponent’s home board before moving other checkers.
- Play the maximum number of dice possible. If either of two unequal dice can be played but not both, play the larger. The board only offers steps that can satisfy these rules. Choose **Pass turn** if the roll has no legal steps at all; otherwise the turn hands over automatically when no steps remain.
- When all your remaining checkers are in your home board, you can bear off. A die can remove a checker exactly that many points from the end, or remove a checker from your farthest occupied point if the die is larger. You can also make a legal move within your home board. A checker hit during bearing off must return home before you can resume bearing off.

The board works with taps or clicks, and with arrow keys followed by Enter or Space. On narrow displays, scroll the board sideways to see every point and tray. When two dice can make the same bear-off step, select which one to spend.

Rules reference: [U.S. Backgammon Federation — Backgammon Basics](https://usbgf.org/backgammon-basics-how-to-play/).
