# Windward Ledger

Take a cutter out of Lantern Dock and earn the **Soundings** charter. Open
`windward-ledger/` under either portal mount. A game starts on the same original
7×6 chart with just the home dock and its neighbors surveyed. Continue opens
your guest save; **Restart voyage** replaces it only after confirmation.

- Use the compass buttons by mouse, touch, or Tab and Enter/Space. Sail to an
  orthogonally adjacent charted water or coastal square. Each arrival surveys
  its four neighbors where they exist. Mountains block travel; `?` marks an
  uncharted square. The symbols and text distinguish the cutter, coast flags,
  your ports, and the home dock.
- You have **two sail steps and two orders each season**. Establish a port
  while moored at a coastal flag (maximum two), then assign it to make one
  good. Founding, changing the assignment, loading a crate, and delivering use
  one order each. An idle port makes nothing. When you end a season, every
  working port makes one crate, or two if assigned its local specialty, up to
  four crates of each good in storage. The next season restores both allowances;
  neither unused steps nor unused orders carry over.
- Load from the stock of the port where you are moored. The cutter can carry
  **four crates** in any mix. **Deliver cargo** at Lantern Dock unloads the whole
  hold. Kelp earns **2** points per crate, dye **3**, and cedar **4**. To win, deliver
  at least **one crate of each** and earn **18 charter points**. If you end season
  **22** without both, the charter expires; that final bell does not make stock.
- **Maritime cues** are off on arrival. Opt in on the voyage page for quiet
  synthesized sounds on accepted sails and deliveries. The setting is for the
  current page, and the cues make no network requests.

This is a short, deterministic supply-route puzzle on a fictional coast, not a
historical settlement simulation. There are no rivals, conquest, labor systems,
or purchases. Charter points have no cash value. The chart, cutter, textile
surfaces, catalog vignette and two cues are original to Jade Court.
[OpenCol's pinned README](https://github.com/OpenCol/win-port/tree/8a9eb7b22a788a54eb7e5e253785c75293f4987c)
describes a decompiled Windows port that requires commercial game inputs, not a
redistributable native web engine. No redistribution rights were verified.
Windward Ledger neither uses nor ships OpenCol code or commercial materials,
including Amiga disk artwork or audio, and does not run or import those files.

See the [solo API](portal-api.md#solo-saves) for the action fields and privacy contract.
