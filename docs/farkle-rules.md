# Farkle rules

Farkle is a push-your-luck game for two to six players. Use a private
invitation for a table of friends, or fill the other seats with bots.

1. Begin your turn by rolling six dice.
2. Select one or more dice that score **on this roll**, then choose **Keep**.
   Every selected die must be part of a scoring combination. You can leave
   scoring dice unselected; those dice can be rolled again. You can only
   keep one selection per roll, and dice kept on different rolls never
   combine.
3. Choose **Roll** to risk your current turn points on the remaining dice,
   or **Bank** to add your turn points to your total and pass the turn.
   Your first bank must be at least **500** points. Later banks can be any
   positive amount.
4. If you keep all dice available on a roll, you have **hot dice**. You can
   choose to roll all six again or bank if you qualify.
5. A roll with no scoring selection is a **Farkle**. You lose all unbanked
   points from the turn and play passes automatically. Banked points stay.

| Selected dice from one roll | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| One `1` / one `5` | 100 / 50 |
| Three `1`s | 300 |
| Three `2`s, `3`s, `4`s, `5`s, or `6`s | 200, 300, 400, 500, or 600 |
| Four / five / six of a kind | 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000 |
| Straight (`1` through `6`) | 1,500 |
| Three different pairs | 1,500 |
| Four of a kind and a different pair | 1,500 |
| Two different triples | 2,500 |

Use the matching count for each face you select: four `5`s score 1,000,
not a triple plus a single. A full six-dice special takes precedence over
its component groups. For example, if you select three `2`s and one `5`,
you score 250. Including an unrelated `6` would invalidate that selection.

Banking **10,000** or more starts the **final round**. Each other player
gets one last turn, in seat order. The highest banked total wins; tied
leaders share the win. A turn has a 24-roll safety limit: if the last roll
scores, keep your selection and bank instead of rolling again.
